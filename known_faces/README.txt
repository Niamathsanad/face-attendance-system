PUT PEOPLE'S PHOTOS HERE.  The system enrolls them automatically.

Easy ways to name things (any one of these works):
  Murtaza.jpg                      -> person "Murtaza" (ID is created automatically)
  Niamatullah Hasand.png           -> person "Niamatullah Hasand"
  Ali (1).jpg  Ali (2).jpg  ...    -> several photos of "Ali" (more photos = better recognition)
  Sara\1.jpg  Sara\2.jpg           -> a sub-folder named "Sara" with her photos
  CS-001__Murtaza.jpg              -> you choose the ID: ID, two underscores, then the name

Rules: one clear face per photo, looking at the camera, good light.
When does it enroll?  (1) every time you start  python app.py
                      (2) when you click "Sync from known_faces folder" on the Registered people page.
People who are already enrolled are skipped. By putting a photo here you confirm the person agreed to it.
You may delete the photos afterwards: only numeric face templates are kept in the database.
